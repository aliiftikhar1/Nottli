# Nottli Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Publisher: Ali Iftikhar · Contact: alijaan.coder@gmail.com

## Summary

Nottli stores everything on your PC. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data, and Nottli contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What Nottli stores, and where

| Data | Where | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notes, sticky notes, preferences, window positions | Local SQLite database in your Windows user profile (`%APPDATA%`) | Always, as you use the app |
| Clipboard history (text, images, rich text, file paths) | Local database and image files in your user profile (`%APPDATA%`, `%LOCALAPPDATA%`) | Only after you turn clipboard history on and accept the in-app notice. Off by default. |

Nottli makes no network connections of its own. Your data never leaves your device through Nottli.

## Clipboard history

- Off by default; nothing is read until you opt in.
- You can pause, turn off, delete single items, or clear everything at any time.
- Retention is configurable (7, 30, 90 days or forever).
- Nottli tries to skip passwords and secrets and honours the Windows "exclude from clipboard history" flags, but detection is best-effort and may miss things.

## Your control

Delete content inside the app, or remove the data folders in `%APPDATA%` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%` (folder named after the app identifier) to erase everything. Uninstalling does not remove them automatically.

## Children

Nottli is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
